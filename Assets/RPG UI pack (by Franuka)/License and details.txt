Included are UI assets in three sizes: 100% (16x16px grid), 200% (32x32px grid) and 300% (48x48px grid), in .png format, transparent background. There's also three .ttf pixel fonts, for texts and titles.

FONTS:
------
* In terms of font size, 'FantasyRPGtext' should be used at multiples of 12, 'FantasyRPGtitle' at multiples of 17, and 'FantasyRPGtitleOutline' at multiples of 20. All this while at 72PPI. This is important to retain the pixel look, since these are not vectorial fonts. Anti-aliasing should be disabled.
