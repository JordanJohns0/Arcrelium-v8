# Equipment Slot Archetypes

Reference for designing equipment cards and triggers.

---

## Head — Intelligence / Magic / Perception

The mind slot. Cards here represent knowledge, awareness, and magical ability.

**Card themes:**
- Enchantments (buffs that enhance other actions)
- Debuffs (stun, blind, confuse — targeting the enemy's mind)
- Scouting (reveal hexes, see through fog, expose hidden enemies)
- Spell effects (ranged magic, area control)
- Card manipulation (peek at draw pile, rearrange top cards)

**Trigger themes:**
- Start of turn: reveal nearby enemies, gain temporary insight
- On card played: chance to duplicate, reduce cost of next card
- On damage taken: reflect mental damage, resist debuffs

---

## Chest — Defense / Resilience

The survivability slot. Cards here keep you alive through raw toughness.

**Card themes:**
- Armour cards (gain block — passive protection)
- Taunt / provoke (force enemies to target you)
- Self-heal (restore health)
- Damage mitigation (reduce incoming damage for a turn)
- Health buffs (increase max health or current health)
- Thorns (deal damage back when hit)

**Trigger themes:**
- Start of turn: gain block, regenerate health
- On damage taken: heal, gain block, reflect damage
- End of turn: recover from status effects

---

## Feet — Movement / Positioning

The mobility slot. Cards here control where you are on the board.

**Card themes:**
- Movement cards (walk, jog, run, sprint — move 1-4 hexes)
- Flank (move and gain bonus on next attack)
- Retreat / disengage (move without triggering reactions)
- Terrain traversal (ignore obstacles, move through occupied hexes)
- Chase (move toward a specific enemy)
- Reposition (swap positions with an ally)

**Trigger themes:**
- Start of turn: gain extra movement range
- On card played: move 1 hex for free
- End of turn: auto-reposition to safest adjacent hex

---

## Hand — Weapons / Offense (2 slots)

The attack slot. Cards here are what you hit people with. Two slots means you can dual-wield or go weapon + shield.

**Card themes:**
- Melee attacks (short range, high damage)
- Ranged attacks (long range, moderate damage)
- Shield / block cards (gain block — active protection)
- Weapon abilities (特殊 moves unique to a weapon type)
- Combo starters (enable follow-up attacks)
- Multi-hit (attack multiple times for less damage each)

**Trigger themes:**
- On card played: bonus damage on next attack, apply bleed
- On deal damage: draw a card, gain block
- Start of turn: gain temporary attack bonus

---

## Trinket — Utility / Wildcard (3 slots)

The flex slot. Three slots and no theme constraint means these fill gaps and enable combos.

**Card themes:**
- Healing (restore health, remove status effects)
- Card draw (draw extra cards)
- Cost reduction (play cards cheaper)
- Resource generation (gain temporary mana, free plays)
- Combo enablers (cards that synergy with other equipment)
- Unique / niche abilities (ones that don't fit elsewhere)

**Trigger themes:**
- Any timing — trinkets are the most flexible for triggers
- Start of turn: draw a card, gain block, heal
- On card played: reduce cost of next card, duplicate effect
- On damage taken: heal, gain block, counter-attack
- End of turn: prepare next turn's first card

---

## Design Principles

1. **One identity per slot.** Head = brain, Chest = toughness, Feet = legs, Hand = weapons, Trinket = everything else. If a card doesn't fit, it probably belongs on a different slot.

2. **Hands are the primary offence.** Most damage comes from hand equipment. Other slots provide support, utility, and defence.

3. **Trinkets enable builds.** Three slots with no theme constraint means trinkets are where combo pieces and build-defining effects live.

4. **Triggers scale with slots.** A single trigger on a trinket is fine. Multiple triggers across all equipment should create interesting interactions, not overwhelming noise.

5. **Block cards can exist on both Chest and Hand.** Chest = passive armour block (Fortify, Iron Skin). Hand = active shield block (Parry, Shield Wall). Same mechanic, different flavour.
